# Asset provenance

All four raster source images were generated for this project with the **built-in imagegen tool**, on 11 September 2026. No external stock photography or client assets are used. All depicted buildings are fictional concepts and are labeled as such in the interface. The brand is a temporary demo identity, not a claim about a real company.

Source PNGs remain in the original imagegen output directory under the operator's Codex home; deployed code references only workspace assets. WebP optimization and the social derivative use Sharp. Diagrams and PDFs are schematic demonstrations, not verified engineering documents; they intentionally do not specify construction dimensions or certified reinforcement designs.

Geist Sans and Geist Mono are provided through `next/font/google`, downloaded at build time and self-hosted. UI icons come from Lucide. The engineering story is an original React SVG illustration; no Spline or third-party model is loaded.

## Exact generation prompts

### The Frame

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: architectural editorial website hero, wide landscape 1536x1024. Create an original photorealistic architectural concept photograph of a monumental exposed reinforced concrete building under construction, viewed dramatically from ground level looking up from its corner. Powerful repeating horizontal floor slabs extending toward right, rhythmic concrete columns, subtle steel reinforcement at top, warm pale concrete, dark interior recesses, expansive pale warm grey sky on left third. Sophisticated architectural magazine photography, quiet overcast daylight, subtle film grain, restrained warm monochrome palette, rigorous plausible structural geometry. No people, no cranes, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is a fictional concept structure, not an identifiable real project.

### Courtyard House

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original architectural editorial photograph-like concept rendering, wide landscape. A refined mid-rise courtyard building with pale board-formed concrete structural frame, deeply recessed glazing, a central courtyard and a single olive tree, viewed from a shaded colonnade. Calm Mediterranean light, tactile concrete, dark window frames, muted warm grey palette, architectural magazine quality, precise plausible geometry, no people no text no logos. Fictional architectural concept for an engineering portfolio.

### Civic Exchange

Use case: stylized-concept. Original fictional architectural concept photograph for premium structural engineering editorial portfolio, landscape. A monumental low-rise civic commercial building, warm exposed concrete, deep repeating double-height colonnade, cantilevered roof, broad empty stone forecourt, oblique perspective. Quiet natural morning sunlight, muted warm neutrals, fine realistic concrete texture, extremely elegant architectural magazine composition, believable structural design. No people, text, logos, watermarks, or recognizable real buildings.

### Northlight Campus

Use case: stylized-concept. Original fictional architectural concept photograph, landscape, a refined educational campus of three-story exposed pale concrete buildings with slender vertical fins, covered walkways and a single low landscaped garden. Perspective from pedestrian level, late afternoon diffuse light, warm grey and graphite palette, strong horizontal composition, realistic concrete texture, quiet premium architectural magazine image, plausible structural geometry. No people, text, logos or watermark. Not an identifiable actual building.
